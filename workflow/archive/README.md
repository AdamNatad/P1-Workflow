# Archive

The read-only past. Nothing here authorizes work, and nothing here is edited except to fix a broken
link. Old material always comes here, never to a new folder.

- `<milestone>/`: a finished milestone's records, moved from `../tasks/<milestone>/`, each with its
  final status (`completed`, `superseded` or `retired`).
- `notes/`: retired notes from `../notes/`, with status `retired`.
- `pending/`: proposals the owner turned down or replaced, with status `superseded`.
- `changelog/` and `history/`: when a ledger grows long, its oldest whole entries move here, newest
  first, so the ledger stays quick to read. The ledger says where its older entries are.
